Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026 | Brand: DispatchBox (Dispatch Box)

Mobile Information & SMS Messaging Non-Sharing Commitment

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are strictly confidential and will never be sold, rented, leased, or disclosed to any third parties, data brokers, or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.

1. Information We Collect

At DispatchBox, we collect personal information necessary to deliver field service management software, schedule technicians, process estimates and invoices, and send customer service notifications:

  • Contact Information: First and last name, business name, service address, email address, and mobile phone number.
  • Service & Dispatch Data: Work order requests, job notes, equipment records, and appointment scheduling details.
  • Mobile Data & Communication Preferences: Mobile phone numbers provided via public service booking forms, quote acceptance, or portal registration for transactional SMS notifications.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP addresses, browser types, and operating system metrics to ensure security and platform reliability.

2. How We Use Mobile Data & Text Messaging

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent collected by DispatchBox are strictly used for transactional, operational, and customer service communications, including:

  • Service appointment confirmations and scheduling reminders.
  • Technician en-route dispatch alerts and estimated arrival times (ETA).
  • Digital quote review links and estimate approval requests.
  • Work order completion summaries and invoice payment links.
  • Direct two-way customer service communications regarding scheduled jobs.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information with trustworthy service providers necessary to operate our platform (such as cloud hosting and carrier telecommunications infrastructure), subject to strict legal confidentiality agreements.

Strict SMS Exclusion: All categories of information sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will never be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Data Protection & Security

We enforce rigorous technical and organizational security controls to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All data transmissions are encrypted using standard SSL/TLS protocols, and database access is governed by strict role-based authorization.

5. Opt-Out Rights & Contact Information

You may opt out of text messaging at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message received from us. For assistance, reply HELP or contact our customer support team directly.
